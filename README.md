# KMS Superstore Sales & Profit Analytics Dashboard

# Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard built from the KMS/Sample Superstore dataset to analyze sales performance, profitability, orders, products, regions, and customer segments.

# Business Objective

The objective of this project is to transform transactional sales data into an interactive management dashboard that helps identify:
Overall sales and profitability performance
Monthly sales trends
High-performing product categories
Most profitable categories
Top-selling products
Regional sales performance
Customer segment contribution.

# Key Insights
1. Technology leads sales
Technology generated approximately $0.84M in sales, making it the strongest-performing category by revenue.
2. Technology also leads profitability
Technology generated approximately $145K in profit, ahead of Office Supplies at approximately $122K and Furniture at approximately $18K.
3. Furniture requires attention
Furniture generated approximately $0.74M in sales but only about $18K in profit. This indicates a much weaker profit contribution relative to its sales volume and warrants further investigation into discounting, product mix, and costs.
4. West is the strongest region
The West generated approximately $0.73M in sales, followed by East at approximately $0.68M.
5. South is the weakest region by sales
The South generated approximately $0.39M in sales, making it the lowest-performing region among the four shown.
6. Consumer is the largest customer segment
Consumer customers account for the largest share of sales, followed by Corporate and Home Office.
7. Overall profitability is positive but has room for improvement
A 12.47% profit margin shows the business is profitable, while the large difference between category sales and profitability suggests opportunities to improve product mix, pricing, discount management, and regional performance.

# Analytical Approach
Loaded and validated the dataset in Power BI.
Verified data types for dates, numeric measures, and identifiers.
Created DAX measures for Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, Total Quantity, and Profit Margin.
Built interactive visuals and slicers
Used a Top N filter to identify the top 10 products by sales.
Designed the dashboard for quick management-level decision making.
